Every year on my birthday, there are a couple of people who message me at 12 AM. Even though I’m about to sleep, I make sure that I respond to each and every single one of them. I make sure that I respond to everyone and tell them how thankful I am, because throughout my life, I’ve always felt that appreciation and love are obligations I have to fulfill, burdens I have to get rid of. That’s just how feelings have been expressed around me my whole life.

If you think of it philosophically, it’s not wrong. But when you think of it in the human sense, it’s the most non-obvious way to deal with emotions.

My friends just video-called me at 12 AM and wished me a happy birthday, and I was like, “Damn, I didn’t know I was this important to people.” I undervalue myself a lot because I set my bar too high, and I’m never able to achieve it. So I think I’m a failure, but I failed to realize that because my bar is so high, I will probably never be content with anything. I will always strive to do better.

A lot of things are intertwined in this super-convoluted brain of mine, and I believe someday I’ll be able to say, “Yes, I am content. Yes, I am not still striving for what I can do tomorrow.” Just one day when maybe I’ll feel like, “Oh, it’s done. Maybe, you know, I have done something sizable, something meaningful with my life,” and just go to rest without thinking a lot. But that is far from near. And I hope I can still take this feeling of not being the best in the world till time immemorial.

I am overly emotional. I am overly excited. I speak out of turn. I speak instinctively. I am impulsive. I am lazy. I am extremely foul-mouthed, and sometimes even very emotionally derogatory because I’m not able to read the room very well. These are all flaws in me that I know exist, and I run after them and try to make sure I do better than I did yesterday, because that is the only thing that will stay with me.

And I need to make sure that whoever is going to spend this time with me also gets the love and affection they deserve, because I am not someone who wants to half-ass anything. And even if I have half-assed something, I am way too tormented because of that half-assing, and that kills me.

So, yeah, this is like a nice, cute-ass birthday dump of everything. Thank you.
